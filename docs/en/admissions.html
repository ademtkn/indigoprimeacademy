Admissions

We are glad that you are considering Indigo Prime Academy. Our admissions team will guide you through every step.

Step by step

How to join us

  1. 1

    Get in touch

    Message us to ask about places in your child’s grade.

  2. 2

    Visit the school

    Tour the building, see a lesson and meet the admissions team.

  3. 3

    Entrance assessment

    A short assessment in English and mathematics, and a friendly interview.

  4. 4

    Submit documents

    Bring the documents from the list below to the school office.

  5. 5

    Welcome to Indigo Prime

    Sign the agreement, receive the uniform and timetable, and get ready for the first day.

Documents

What to prepare

  • Copy of the birth certificate or passport
  • Copies of both parents’ passports
  • Medical record (form 026/u) and vaccination card
  • Personal file and report card from the previous school
  • 3×4 cm photographs, 4 pieces
  • Application form completed at the school office

The final list may change. Please confirm it with the school office before your visit.

Tuition fees

Tuition is set for each academic year. Please contact the admissions office for current fees, discounts and payment options.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Which grades do you accept?

Indigo Prime Academy teaches Grades 7 to 12. Pupils usually join in Grade 7, but transfers to other grades are possible when places are available.

Is there an entrance test?

Yes. Pupils complete a short assessment in English and mathematics and have a friendly interview, so that we can plan the right support.

My child’s English is not strong yet. Can they still apply?

Yes. We look at each pupil’s potential. New pupils can receive extra English support to help them follow Cambridge lessons.

Is the school for girls and boys?

Yes, Indigo Prime Academy is a co-educational school. Girls and boys learn together.

What is the Singapore method?

It is a way of teaching where new ideas move from objects to pictures to symbols, and where pupils work actively in pairs and teams in every lesson.

Can we visit before applying?

Yes, and we recommend it. Message us to agree a convenient day and time to visit and see a lesson.

Come and see a lesson

The best way to understand the Singapore method is to see it. Book a visit, sit in on a lesson and meet our teachers.
