About Us

Indigo Prime Academy is the international high school of the Indigo Academy network in Bishkek, for pupils in Grades 7–12.

Our story

A school made for teenagers

Indigo Prime Academy was founded within Indigo Academy, a network of private schools in Bishkek known for strong results and caring teachers.

Families asked for a school that would take their children from the middle grades to university with an international programme, modern teaching and the values they hold dear. Indigo Prime is our answer: a school designed around the needs, interests and ambitions of teenagers.

We combine the Cambridge curriculum with the Singapore method, so that pupils not only know more but also understand more deeply, think independently and learn to work as a team.

Our mission

To educate curious, knowledgeable and responsible young people who are ready for the best universities and for life in a changing world.

Our vision

To be the leading international high school in Kyrgyzstan, where the world’s best methods meet our culture and values.

Our values

Five words we live by

  1. Curiosityasking why, and searching for answers ourselves.
  2. Excellencehigh standards in every lesson and every task.
  3. Respectfor elders, for classmates and for every person.
  4. Responsibilityfor our learning, our words and our community.
  5. Teamworksucceeding together and helping others grow.

Director’s welcome

Dear parents and pupils

Thank you for your interest in Indigo Prime Academy. Choosing a school for the teenage years is one of the most important decisions a family makes.

Our teachers know every pupil by name. They notice when a pupil is ready for a bigger challenge and when they need a little more support. We measure success not only in exam results but in the confidence with which our pupils speak, the questions they ask and the goals they dare to set.

I warmly invite you to visit us, to see a Singapore-method lesson and to talk with our team. Our doors are always open.

Director, Indigo Prime Academy

Co-education

Girls and boys learning together

Indigo Prime is a mixed school. Teenagers learn side by side, just as they will at university and at work, in an atmosphere of mutual respect and clear rules.

Real teamwork

Mixed teams bring different ideas to every project and discussion.

Respect and clear rules

A clear code of conduct and attentive class teachers keep the school calm and safe.

Confidence for everyone

Every pupil leads, presents and takes part, whatever the subject.

Ready for university

Pupils leave school used to working with everyone, as they will in higher education.
