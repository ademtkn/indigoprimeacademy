School Life

School is more than lessons. It is friendship, projects, competitions and the feeling of belonging to a team.

A typical day

A day at Indigo Prime

  1. 08:00

    Morning welcome

    Pupils arrive and meet their class teacher.

  2. 08:30

    Lessons begin

    Core Cambridge subjects while minds are fresh.

  3. 12:30

    Lunch and break

    Time to eat together, rest and talk with friends.

  4. 13:30

    Afternoon lessons

    Sciences, languages and electives.

  5. 15:30

    Clubs and study

    Clubs, sport, olympiad groups and supervised study.

Times are indicative and may differ by grade.
