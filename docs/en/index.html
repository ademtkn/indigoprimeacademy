Welcome

The teenage years are where futures are built

Between Grade 7 and Grade 12 a child becomes a young adult. These six years decide how a person thinks, studies and works with others. At Indigo Prime Academy we make them count.

Our pupils follow the internationally recognised Cambridge pathway, learn mathematics and every other subject with the Singapore method, and grow in a friendly, respectful community of girls and boys.

How we teach

Two methods, one strong education

Cambridge gives us what to learn and how to measure it. The Singapore method shapes how every lesson is taught.

Cambridge curriculum

An international programme used by schools in more than 160 countries, with clear standards for every subject and every year.

  • Cambridge Lower Secondary, IGCSE and A Level
  • Qualifications recognised by universities worldwide
  • Learning in English

Singapore method

Active lessons where every pupil thinks, talks and solves problems, and mathematics moves from objects to pictures to symbols.

  • Concrete → pictorial → abstract
  • Bar models and problem-solving strategies
  • Pair and team work in every lesson
