School stages

From Grade 7 to graduation

Grades 7–8 · Cambridge Checkpoint

Cambridge Lower Secondary

Pupils strengthen the core subjects and learn how to study: planning, note-taking, research and presenting. The stage ends with Cambridge Checkpoint tests that show each pupil’s strengths.

  • English, mathematics and science to Cambridge standards
  • Digital literacy and computing
  • Project and research weeks
  • Cambridge Checkpoint at the end of the stage

Grades 9–10 · IGCSE exams

Cambridge IGCSE

Pupils choose their IGCSE subjects with the help of teachers and parents. Lessons focus on deep understanding, laboratory work, essay writing and exam skills.

  • Core and elective IGCSE subjects
  • Laboratory and practical work
  • Career guidance and subject choice
  • International IGCSE examinations

Grades 11–12 · AS & A Level exams

Cambridge AS & A Level

In the final two years pupils specialise in three or four subjects at AS and A Level. A Level results are accepted by universities around the world.

  • Three to four specialist subjects
  • University counselling and applications
  • Preparation for IELTS / SAT and the ORT
  • Research projects and leadership
